Privacy policy

story-bot is a personal tool used by one person to upload videos to their own YouTube channel. It only requests permission to upload videos (youtube.upload). It does not read any other Google data.

The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's private server, is used only to upload videos, and is never sold or shared. Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

story-bot uses YouTube API Services: see the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). Access can be removed anytime at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
